## Daily Use

1. **Double-click on the "BSP-G2 Tracker" shortcut** (or on `run-with-server.bat`) — the browser
   opens automatically at <http://localhost:8765>. Close the black window to stop.

2. **Get the Excel file**: use the **"Download from SharePoint"** button in the app (downloads
   through your browser, with your session) or download manually:
   [BSP-G2_Daily_Tracker.xlsx](https://criticalsoftwaresa.sharepoint.com/sites/WRSHALLOWFORD/_layouts/15/download.aspx?UniqueId=107B4AEF-D629-4094-92D1-3F681C4B12EF).
   Better yet: in Teams, in the channel folder where the tracker is, use
   **"Add shortcut to OneDrive"** — the app will automatically pick up
   updates without downloads.

3. **Enter your name** in the field at the top (it's remembered in your browser).

In the top bar you can also **select the Excel file** (from those the app
finds; by default it uses the most recent) and **the sheet** to display — your choices
are remembered. Sheets with a different structure are shown as a simple table.
